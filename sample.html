Batch filming workflow

  1. Choose five briefs you can honestly demonstrate with existing work.
  2. Get permission from anyone visible and remove customer identifiers.
  3. Film vertically in a quiet, well-lit space. Capture a clear spoken introduction and relevant detail shots.
  4. Edit one useful idea per video. Preserve intelligible audio and add readable captions.
  5. Review factual claims and publish when your team can respond to inquiries.
